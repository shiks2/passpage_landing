Data Retention Policy

Company: PassPage Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Version: v1.0.0

DPDP Section 8(6) compliance

Summary

Data lifecycle policy defining retention periods, deletion triggers, and cryptographic erasure procedures across profile data, consent logs, and operational telemetry.

Key Highlights

  • Retention only for required business and legal purposes.
  • Automatic purging on account deletion, direct record deletion, inactivity, or retention expiry.
  • Cryptographic erasure and rolling backup overwrite.
  • Auditability for consent and compliance records.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy standardizes retention, archival, and secure disposal across production databases, telemetry pipelines, cloud infrastructure, and backups in line with DPDP Act Section 8(6).

2. Core Retention Principles

  • Purpose limitation: data kept only as long as required for its original purpose.
  • Storage minimization: avoid indefinite retention and use shortest practical compliant duration.
  • Proactive disposal: erase or irreversibly anonymize after purpose completion or legal retention expiry.

3. Deletion and Cryptographic Erasure Procedures

  • Logical invalidation from active APIs and auth gates.
  • Primary physical purge from operational databases.
  • Backup expungement through rolling encrypted backup cycles.
  • Irreversible anonymization for long-term aggregate telemetry.

4. Legal Holds and Regulatory Exceptions

Records may be temporarily retained beyond baseline schedules for legal obligations, court orders, investigations, or imminent legal claims. Standard schedules resume once the hold is lifted.

5. Policy Governance and Periodic Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Data Protection Officer and Legal Counsel. Updates are published with incremented versioning when legal or regulatory requirements evolve.
